Managers are usually interested in the relationship between [[Sales]], [[Costs]], and [[Net profit]].

Using the equation:
sales - variable expenses - fixed expenses = net income

And expanding it to:
(unit sales price * number of units) - (unit variable cost * number of units) - fixed expenses = net income

To find the break even point we have to equal. $$\textnormal{net income = 0}$$
I used and manipulated the following general formula to derive all the required answers in homework 2.

$$nS - nV - F = I$$
Where:
$$n = \textnormal{Número de unidades}$$
$$S = \textnormal{Ingreso de venta por unidad} $$
$$V = \textnormal{Costo variable por unidad}$$
$$F = \textnormal{Costos fijos}$$
$$I = \textnormal{Ganancias}$$
